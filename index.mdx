Shattered Reality is a Roblox experience built in Luau where one player assumes the role of a powerful **Killer** while up to several others play as **Survivors**. The codebase drives a full game loop — from lobby and intermission through map loading, ability combat, and post-round rewards — using custom networking, server-side anticheat, and a rich VFX pipeline.

These docs cover the game's systems for contributors and developers who want to understand how everything fits together, add new characters, extend the networking layer, or configure moderation tools.
